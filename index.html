Systems Integration

The Spaceport America Cup Rocketry Project brings together propulsion, avionics, structures, recovery, and manufacturing into a single launch vehicle.

My work within Structures Integration focuses on understanding how individual structural components work together to transfer loads through the vehicle while remaining manufacturable and testable.

Rocket Structures Load Paths CAD Integration

SARP / 01

ARES Mount

Mechanical Design · Structural Integration

Designed an ARES mount as part of the vehicle's internal structural architecture. The design required consideration of structural interfaces, load transfer, stiffness, packaging, and manufacturability.

A major consideration was how the mount interfaces with surrounding structural members without creating unnecessary stress concentrations or difficult-to- manufacture geometry.

SolidWorks CAD Structures Integration

SARP / 02

Buckling Test Plan

Structural Analysis · Experimental Testing

Developed a structural buckling test plan to evaluate the behavior of rocket structural components under compressive loading.

The objective is to connect theoretical buckling predictions and numerical analysis with experimental behavior. Test planning includes specimen geometry, loading, boundary conditions, instrumentation, failure criteria, and repeatability.

Buckling Compression Testing Analysis

SARP / 03

Bulkheads

Structural Design · Load Transfer

Worked on rocket bulkhead architecture and the structural interfaces between bulkheads, spars, and other internal components.

Bulkhead design requires balancing stiffness, mass, manufacturing constraints, and the transfer of axial, bending, shear, and localized loads through the vehicle.

Bulkheads Load Paths Composite Structures FEA
